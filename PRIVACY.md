Introduction
LendCircle (“the Platform”) is an AI‑powered peer‑to‑peer lending system. We are committed to protecting user privacy, financial data, and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it.

By using LendCircle, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect
1. User‑Provided Information
Account details (name, email, username)

Financial information required for lending workflows

User‑submitted forms, messages, or documents

Profile and preference settings

2. System‑Generated Information
AI‑generated recommendations or risk assessments

Interaction logs and usage analytics

Session metadata (timestamps, device type, browser type)

3. Technical Information
IP address

Browser and device identifiers

Authentication tokens

Error logs

How We Use Information
We use collected data to:

Operate and improve the LendCircle platform

Facilitate lending workflows and user interactions

Provide AI‑assisted risk evaluation

Maintain security, fraud detection, and compliance

Improve user experience and platform reliability

Communicate updates, alerts, and support messages

We do not sell user data.

Data Sharing
We may share data with:

Service providers (authentication, hosting, analytics)

Financial or regulatory partners when legally required

AI modules that assist with risk evaluation

Law enforcement when required by law

We never share data for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Data Protection
We implement industry‑standard security measures:

Encryption in transit and at rest

Secure authentication and authorization

Access controls and audit logging

Regular security reviews

No system is 100% secure, but we take all reasonable steps to protect user data.

User Rights
Depending on jurisdiction, users may:

Request access to their data

Request deletion or correction

Restrict processing

Export their data

Requests can be submitted through the platform or by contacting the maintainers.

Children’s Privacy
LendCircle is not intended for individuals under 18.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of updated terms.

Contact
For privacy questions or concerns, contact the maintainers privately.
